Learn everyday Chinese handwriting in Regular Script
(<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">楷書</span>)
through the ASK tutorial series.

The tutorials are organised into four sections. You can follow
them in sequence or revisit a particular topic.

## Basic Guides
### ASK000–ASK000.7

Start with the introduction, then explore individual strokes
and stroke order.

Topics include:

- Dots (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">點</span>)
- Horizontal (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">橫</span>) and Vertical (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">豎</span>)
- Throws (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">撇</span>) and Presses (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">捺</span>)
- Lifts (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">挑</span>) and Hooks (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">鉤</span>)
- Bends (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">彎</span>) and Zigzags (<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">折</span>)
- Stroke Order

## Intermediate Guides
### ASK001–ASK040 · Single-Component Characters

Study single-component characters
(<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">獨體字</span>),
with attention to stroke placement, proportion, and balance.

## Advanced Guides
### ASK041–ASK100 · Composite Characters

Explore how components fit together within composite characters
(<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">合體字</span>).

## Structural Patterns
### ASK101–ASK150 · Provisional

Explore character structure
(<span class="chinese-font" lang="zh-Hant">間架結構</span>)
and recurring arrangements of strokes and components.

The topics and sequence in this section are still being developed.
